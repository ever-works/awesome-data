## Overview

AI Tools Radar is a multilingual directory for discovering and comparing AI products, model profiles, agent skills, and prompts.

## Features

- Searchable AI tool and model directories
- Agent Skills and prompt discovery
- Source-linked news and newsletter archives
- Browsing in ten languages

## Pricing

Public browsing and newsletter subscription are free. Vendor directory submissions and promotion are separate paid services.
